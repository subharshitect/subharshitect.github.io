01Work

I work on the systems side of machine learning. My interests include memory management during inference, KV caches and paging, compressing model parameters, and finding out where a system actually spends its time by tracing it end to end.

  • FocusKV-cache management and paging · parameter deltas and compression · trace-driven performance analysis
  • ToolsPyTorch · CUDA · Triton · eBPF · perf
  • Methodmeasure, reduce, remain
  • ProjectsTwo or three projects, each with a one-line result and a link.

A question I’m thinking about

  • When does reducing memory use make a system harder to reason about?
  • When a model is compressed, which losses never show up in the benchmarks we use to check it?
  • How much of a profile can I trust once the profiler itself is part of the run?

  • What is the right unit to evict from a KV cache: a token, a block, or a request?
  • Below what model size does inference stop being about the GPU at all?
